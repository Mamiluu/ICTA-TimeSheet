Your payment form, exactly as your office prints it.

If people are paid for the days they attend, the sheet is the evidence. So we print the form you already use: your letterhead, nine days to a page, rows nobody used struck through, your own line at the foot.

Behind it is what paper can't do. The preparer sends it, the authorising officer certifies it, finance verifies it. Each step is sealed, and odd rates or late additions are flagged for the approver to look at.

What the record shows, after one payment sheet.

Every step below is written to a log where each entry carries a fingerprint of the one before it. Changing an old entry breaks every entry after it.

  1. Day 1, 08:02Ruth opens the link on her phone and signs for Day 1.
  2. Day 1, 09:12Faith signs. Hassan doesn't, and his box stays empty.
  3. Day 1, 17:30The day closes. Nobody can sign for it any more, unless the organiser reopens it, and that is recorded too.
  4. Day 5, 16:10The preparer sends the sheet to the authorising officer. The system points out one person whose rate differs from the others.
  5. Day 6, 09:30The officer reads the flag, certifies, and the sheet is sealed. A QR goes on the printed copy.
  6. Day 8, 11:05Finance verifies against the seal. Payment goes ahead.

Straight answers.

Do people need to install an app?

No. They open a link in the phone's browser, type the number they were registered with, and sign.

What if there is no signal at the door?

Door scanning keeps working without signal and sends what it collected once the phone is back online.

Can a signature be added after the day is over?

Not by itself. A day closes when its hours end. The organiser can reopen it for a stated period if someone really was there, and the reopening is written to the record with a reason.

Who can see our people's details?

Only people inside your organisation, and only what the access you gave them allows. Another organisation can never see yours. The platform team can look inside only if your administrator approves it, for a set number of hours, read-only, and never at attendee records. Each look is logged where you can see it.

Can we use our own name, logo and colours?

Yes. Your letterhead, logo, colours and the line at the foot of printed sheets are yours, and you can run it on your own web address.

What does it cost?

Organisations are added on a plan the platform owner sets. Ask to add yours and you will get an answer by email.

Want your office to stop passing round a clipboard?

Ask to add your organisation. We confirm your email, look at the request, and set you up with your own name, logo and colours.
