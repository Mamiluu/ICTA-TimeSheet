Why it exists

A paper sheet is easy to pad and hard to prove.

When pay depends on a list of names, the list becomes the weak point. Public audits keep finding the same gap between who signed and who was paid.

  • Signed on the day, by the person. A name cannot be added afterwards without leaving a trace.
  • Only people on the list can sign, using the phone number they were registered with.
  • Approved before it is paid. The officer and finance each seal their step.

What is inside

Everything an attendance office actually needs.

Built around how paper registers are used in real offices, then made hard to fake.

One signature for every day

Multi-day events get a column for each day. People sign for a day on that day. Missed days stay visibly empty instead of being filled in later.

A tamper-evident activity log

Every sign-in, change and approval is written to a chain where each entry fingerprints the one before it. An edit made later shows.

Payment sheets

The paper form you already use. Done properly.

When people are paid for the days they attend, the sheet is the evidence. Ours prints as the same familiar form, but it is signed on phones and approved before anyone is paid.

  • Prints like your paper sheet. Letterhead, nine days to a page, unused rows struck through, your own footer line.
  • A real approval path. Prepared, certified by the authorising officer, verified by finance. Each step is sealed.
  • Automatic checks. Odd rates, late additions and gaps are flagged for the approver to look at.
  • Access you control. Only the people you give payment access to can see or prepare a sheet.

Trust

Careful with people's information, by design.

Attendance records hold names, phone numbers and signatures. We treat that as a duty, not a feature.

01

Records that show tampering

Sealed sheets, a chained activity log and an integrity check on every report mean quiet edits do not go unnoticed.

02

Every organisation is fenced off

Each organisation's people, events and records are kept apart. One organisation can never see another's. We test this on every change.

03

Support only with your consent

The platform team can look inside an organisation only if its administrator approves, for a set number of hours, read-only, and never at attendee records. Every look is logged to you.
