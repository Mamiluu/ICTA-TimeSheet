What is inside

Everything an attendance office needs.

Built around how paper registers are used in real offices, then made hard to fake.

One signature for every day

Multi-day events get a column for each day. Missed days stay visibly empty instead of being filled in later.

Sealed sheets

Every finished sheet carries a QR that anyone can scan to check it against the record.

A tamper-evident log

Every sign-in, change and approval is written to a chain where each entry fingerprints the one before it.

Door scanning

Scan passes at the entrance. It keeps working when the signal drops and catches up when it returns.

Certificates

When an event ends, attendees can collect a certificate that can itself be verified.

Your name and colours

Your logo, letterhead, accent colour and printed footer line, on your own web address if you wish.

Payment sheets

Nobody is paid until the right people have signed off.

When people are paid for the days they attend, the sheet is the evidence. It prints as the form you already use, and behind it is an approval path paper can't give you.

  • Prints like your paper sheet. Letterhead, nine days to a page, unused rows struck through, your own footer line.
  • A real approval path. Prepared, certified by the authorising officer, verified by finance. Each step is sealed.
  • Automatic checks. Odd rates, late additions and gaps are flagged for the approver to look at.
  • Access you control. Only the people you give payment access to can see or prepare a sheet.

Trust

Careful with people's information, by design.

Attendance records hold names, phone numbers and signatures. We treat that as a duty.

Records that show tampering

Sealed sheets, a chained activity log and an integrity check on every report mean quiet edits do not go unnoticed.

Every organisation is fenced off

Each organisation's people, events and records are kept apart. One organisation can never see another's.

Support only with your consent

The platform team can look inside only if your administrator approves it, for a set time, read-only, and never at attendee records.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do people need to install an app?

No. They open a link in the phone's browser, type the number they were registered with, and sign.

What if there is no signal at the door?

Door scanning keeps working without signal and sends what it collected once the phone is back online.

Can a signature be added after the day is over?

Not by itself. A day closes when its hours end. The organiser can reopen it for a stated period, and the reopening is written to the record with a reason.

Who can see our people's details?

Only people inside your organisation, and only what their access allows. Another organisation can never see yours. The platform team can look inside only if your administrator approves it, read-only, and never at attendee records.

Can we use our own name, logo and colours?

Yes. Your letterhead, logo, colours and the line at the foot of printed sheets are yours, and you can use your own web address.

What does it cost?

Organisations are added on a plan the platform owner sets. Ask to add yours and you will get an answer by email.

Ready to put the clipboard down?

Ask to add your organisation. We confirm your email, review the request, and set you up with your own name, logo and colours.
