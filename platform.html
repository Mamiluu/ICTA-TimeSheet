Audit trail integrity

When you press the button below, the latest entry of the tamper-evident audit trail, covering every department, is emailed to you. That email is a copy nobody can change from inside the system. Paste a hash from one of those emails to check that nothing up to that point was altered or removed.

Help from the platform team

If something is wrong and the platform team needs to look into it, they must ask you first. If you approve, they get a read-only view of your overview pages (departments, administrators and the activity log) for the hours they asked for. They never see attendee names, phone numbers, signatures or event records, and they cannot change anything. Every page they open is written to your Audit trail. You can end it at any moment.
