Daymark: three logo ideas

None of these is a circle or an S, so none resembles the Sajili badge. A daymark is the fixed beacon on a coast that sailors steer by, and also simply a mark for the day. All three are drawn as vector shapes with blended colour.

1. The beacon

A tower of five stacked bands, one for each day, tapering up to a lit top. Like the striped markers on a coast. Colours blend from orange through magenta and violet to teal.

2. The striped D

The letter D built from four day-bars, each a shade darker, with a white tick running through them. A confident monogram with the days built in.

3. The dayboard

A diamond like the navigation boards sailors steer by, in four blended colours, with a tick cut through the centre. Simple, geometric, easy to stamp on paper.
